# Quadrant: Tutorial

**A lesson.** Compass: informs *action* · serves *acquisition of skill*.

The reader is a learner in your hands. They do not yet know what they need to
know, so they cannot judge what to skip — you make every choice for them. The
measure of a tutorial is not what it covers. It is whether the learner reaches
the end having succeeded.

## Craft

- **Guarantee the result.** The tutorial works, every time, for every reader who
  follows it. Run it end to end before shipping it. A step that works on your
  machine and nowhere else has failed.
- **Show something real, early.** The learner needs visible evidence they are
  getting somewhere before their patience runs out.
- **Stay concrete.** Particular commands on particular files with particular
  output. General principles belong in Explanation.
- **Choose for them.** One path. No alternatives, no options, no "you could also".
  Every branch you offer is a decision the learner is not yet equipped to make.
- **Narrate what to notice.** "The `id` field is new — that's the record we just
  created." Learners see what they are told to see.
- **Reassure at anything surprising.** Warnings, slow steps, output that looks
  wrong but isn't: name them before the learner hits them and assumes they broke it.
- **Repeat yourself.** Repetition teaches. It is not redundancy here.
- **Point away for the why.** A sentence of context is fine; a paragraph is an
  Explanation trying to get in. Link to it.

## Register

**Plain and economical — not telegraphic.** A lesson works through the learner's
confidence, and confidence needs connective prose. Keep articles and full
sentences. Write in the first person plural: *we'll add a handler now*. Signpost
what is coming and what just happened.

Cut padding and hedges — LANGUAGE AND STYLE in `SKILL.md` still applies. Do not cut
the connective tissue that carries the learner from one step to the next, and do not
cut deliberate repetition: repetition teaches.

## Title

*Getting started with X* · *Your first X* · *A tutorial on X*

## Done when

- You ran every step end to end in this repo, and each output shown is the
  output you got. A step you could not run is marked untested and named to the
  user.
- Every step is concrete, and every step presents exactly one path.
- Explanation and machinery detail live in their own documents, linked from here.
- Full sentences in first person plural, a signpost at each step; deliberate
  repetition kept, padding cut.
