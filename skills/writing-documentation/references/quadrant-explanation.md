# Quadrant: Explanation

**A discussion.** Compass: informs *cognition* · serves *acquisition of understanding*.

The reader wants to understand, and they are not at the keyboard. They may be
reading on the train, deciding whether to adopt the thing, or trying to work out
why it behaves as it does. This is the quadrant that can be read away from the
software entirely.

## Craft

- **Answer *why*, not *how*.** Why the design went this way. What it was weighed
  against. What it costs.
- **Supply context.** History, constraints at the time, the alternative that was
  rejected and the reason. This is the material that exists nowhere else — the
  code records the decision, never the deliberation.
- **Make connections.** Relate the topic to neighbouring ideas, to other systems,
  to how things are done elsewhere. Joining things together is the work.
- **Admit opinion and perspective.** Explanation is the one quadrant where *we
  chose X because we think Y matters more than Z* belongs. Attribute it honestly.
- **Take a bounded topic.** *About the retry model* is a discussion. *About the
  system* is a book.
- **Instruct in nothing.** The moment steps appear, a how-to has escaped into the
  wrong document.

## Register

**Plain and economical — not telegraphic.** An argument needs conjunctions.
Discussion depends on *because*, *whereas*, *which means that* — the connective
words telegraphic prose strips out. Keep full sentences and paragraphs.

Cut padding and hedges — the **Shared rules** in `SKILL.md` still apply. Do not cut
the reasoning that holds the argument together.

## Title

*About X* · *Why X works this way* · *Understanding X*

## Done when

- The document answers why, and a reader learns something they could not get
  from reading the source.
- Any procedure the topic implies lives in its own how-to, linked from here.
- The topic is bounded, and the document stays inside it.
